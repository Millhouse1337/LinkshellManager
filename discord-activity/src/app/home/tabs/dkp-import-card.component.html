Moving from another tracker? Upload a spreadsheet of your existing DKP and every member's balance comes with you. You'll review every row before anything changes.

ACCEPTS .XLSX · .CSV
🧭

How it works

  1. 1

    Download the template

    It's shown at the bottom of this page, so you can see the layout before you open it.

  2. 2

    Fill it in

    One row per member: their name, up to two alts, and their DKP. Spare ✦ rows are skipped.

  3. 3

    Upload and preview

    Every row shows as an update, a new member, or skipped, with its old and new balance.

  4. 4

    Import

    Nothing changes until you press Import.

The columns

Member Name

Required. The member's main character.

Alt 1 Alt 2

Optional. Saved as the alts of a member the import adds.

Current DKP

Required. What they can spend right now. A negative number imports as 0.

Total DKP

Optional. Everything they've earned.

Total DKP Spent

Optional. Everything they've spent.

Biddable DKP

In the template, but ignored by the import: the app works it out itself.
